# Design System: Laneway

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The tidy colleague"**

Laneway should feel like working next to someone calm and organized who notices things and sometimes raises an eyebrow. People spend hours on a board, so the board is quiet. It uses warm olive-gray surfaces, one lime accent that only marks what you can act on or where you are, and plain copy. The personality lives at the edges, meaning empty states, onboarding, sign-in, 404, and errors. Those screens get Cobble the mascot, a condensed display headline, a soft lime-tinted backdrop, and copy with a little cheek. Both halves share one system: the same family, radii, neutrals, and accent.

The base is shadcn/ui on Base UI. We keep its structure, copy-in components themed through CSS variables, and make it ours through tokens, type, spacing, radius, and a few signature details: the card face, the tinted list, the lime list header, and the mascot. There is one comfortable density. Only a coarse pointer changes sizes.

Laneway must not look like stock shadcn: Geist everywhere, zero-chroma grays, a black primary, a faint gray focus ring, dashed-box empty states with an icon tile. It also rules out purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, Corporate Memphis people, sparkle AI icons, colored icon tiles, a different hue per stat tile, tilted cards at rest, big image covers, flat cartoon people, Trello blue, and lane-marker or street-sign motifs.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm olive-gray neutrals at hue 105 to 110 in both themes. Dark mode is a warm near-black, never navy.
- One lime accent, always a fill with dark olive text on top.
- Board, list, and card separate by lightness steps plus a faint card shadow. Cards have no border.
- Soft, nested radii: 0.875rem cards inside 1.375rem lists, 1.5rem dialogs, pill chips.
- Instrument Sans as the only face.
- Short motion that settles and never bounces.
- WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark from day one, with AAA where it costs little.

## Colors

A moss-and-lime palette: low-chroma warm grays that read like stone, with one bright lime that reads like moss growing where it matters. Every color token is OKLCH and inside sRGB. Each token above has a light value and a `-dark` value. They map onto shadcn's CSS variable names, plus Laneway's own `list`, `selection`, `success`, `warning`, `info`, and their `-foreground` pairs.

### Primary

- **Lime** (`primary`): the accent, the same in both themes. It fills the primary button, the current board in the sidebar (`sidebar-accent`), and the header of the list that holds focus. Text on it is always **Dark Olive** (`primary-foreground`). The light lime button is only 1.19:1 against the board. That passes because its label identifies it.
- **Faint Lime** (`accent`): shadcn's hover tint for menu items, ghost buttons, and rows. It is not the brand accent.
- **Lime Selection** (`selection`): selected text and selected rows.

### Neutral

- **Stone Board** (`background`): the board and page background.
- **Stone List** (`list`): a list surface, one step lighter than the board in dark mode and one step darker in light mode.
- **Paper Card** (`card`, `popover`): cards, dialogs, popovers, and menus.
- **Ink** (`foreground`): body text and titles.
- **Moss Gray** (`muted-foreground`): meta text, counts, and placeholder text.
- **Stone Wash** (`secondary`, `muted`): secondary buttons, the observer strip, +N discs, and quiet fills.
- **Hairline** (`border`): dividers and the dark-mode ring on popovers and dialogs.
- **Field Edge** (`input`): input borders. It sits darker than shadcn's default (L 0.61 light, 0.545 dark) so a field's edge reaches 3:1.
- **Sidebar Stone** (`sidebar`): the sidebar, a step darker than the board in dark mode.

### Focus

- **Olive Ring** (`ring`): the focus ring in light mode. Lime on the light board is only 1.2:1, so light mode uses dark olive. Dark mode uses lime.

### Status

- `destructive`, `success`, `warning`, and `info` each work as text on a card and as a fill with their `-foreground`. An overdue date reads in `destructive`. A complete due date, and a checklist with every check item checked, read in `success`.

### Named rules

**The Fill Rule.** Lime is a fill with dark olive text. Never lime text, never a lime outline on a light surface, never a lime icon on its own.

**The Where-You-Are Rule.** Lime marks what you can act on and where you are: the primary button, the current board, the focused list's header, the dark-mode focus ring, the lime wash on a moved card. Nothing decorative is lime. The focused list header goes further than "interactive state only". The user chose that knowing it.

**The Named Color Rule.** Color is never the only signal. A named label shows its name. A color-only label is a swatch whose tooltip and accessible name are the color name, and the label picker prints the names beside the swatches.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Instrument Sans at width 80 (with `next/font` fallback metrics)
**Body Font:** Instrument Sans (with `next/font` fallback metrics)

**Character:** One soft grotesque does everything. Edge headlines get their voice from the condensed width and a bigger size, not from a second face. Instrument Sans is free and self-hosted through `next/font` with the `wdth` axis, and its fallback metrics mean it loads without layout shift.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (650, `clamp(2rem, 1.4rem + 4cqi, 2.75rem)`, 1.05, width 80): edge headlines only, on empty states, onboarding, sign-in, 404, and errors. It is the only fluid role.
- **Title** (600, 1.375rem, 1.25): dialog and page titles.
- **Heading** (600, 0.9375rem, 1.3): list headers and dialog sections.
- **Card** (500, 0.9375rem, 1.35): card titles.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.55): descriptions and comments, capped at 68ch with `text-wrap: pretty`.
- **Meta** (500, 0.8125rem, 1.35, 0.005em): labels, due dates, counts, and `kbd` hints, with tabular numerals.

### Named rules

**The One Family Rule.** No display face and no mono. If something needs more voice, change width or size.

**The Fixed Board Rule.** Board and dialog sizes are fixed. Only the display role uses `clamp()`, it starts from rem, and it never uses viewport units alone.

Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. The scale survives 320px with the 1.4.12 text spacing overrides in both themes. The display headline is the tightest fit.

## Layout

Everything that sets layout or size is in rem: breakpoints, spacing, control heights, widths, radii, type, and mascot sizes. px stays only for detail that shouldn't grow with text: 1px borders, the 2px focus and avatar rings, shadow offsets and blur, and the 1.5px icon stroke. The due date calendar's day cells are the one exception, described under Card detail. A rem is 16px at the default text size. If someone sets their browser's text size to 200%, the breakpoints move with it, so a 1280px laptop gets the phone board. That's intended.

The base unit is 0.25rem (4px). Spacing steps are 0.25, 0.375, 0.5, 0.75, 0.875, 1, 1.125, 1.5, 1.75, 2, and 2.5rem. Only 0.375, 0.875, and 1.125rem sit off the 4px grid.

### Breakpoints

Width is the only thing that changes the layout. There are two viewport media queries, Tailwind's defaults: `sm` at 40rem and `lg` at 64rem. There's no height rule and no landscape rule, so a phone in landscape gets the desktop board.

|               | Below 40rem                                      | 40rem to 64rem                                           | 64rem and up                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Board         | Phone carousel                                   | Desktop board                                            | Desktop board                           |
| Card detail   | Full screen                                      | Centered dialog                                          | Centered dialog                         |
| Members panel | Full screen                                      | 22.5rem, slides over the board's right side with a scrim | 22.5rem beside the board, which narrows |
| Sidebar       | Slides in from a menu button in the board header | Same                                                     | Docked, and the user can collapse it    |
| Share         | Icon only                                        | Icon and label                                           | Icon and label                          |

The board switch is a media query, not a container query, so opening the sidebar or the members panel never turns the board into a carousel. 40rem is the first Tailwind step where two 18rem lists and three gaps fit. Container queries stay inside lists and cards. shadcn's `useIsMobile` hardcodes 768px. Change it to 64rem in the copied-in hook.

Rules about touch key off `pointer: coarse` or `hover: none`, not width. Those rules cover the 2.75rem control sizes, the card's menu button, the grip, and the "Move…" bottom sheet. An iPad gets the desktop board with touch-friendly cards.

### Board

Desktop board: lists are 18rem wide with a 0.75rem gap, and the same 0.75rem is the board's side gutter. The board scrolls sideways, and each list scrolls its own cards. List padding is 0.5rem, cards sit 0.5rem apart, and parts inside a card sit 0.5rem apart.

Phone board: one list per screen in a snap carousel (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`). A list is the board's width minus 3.5rem, which leaves a peek of the next list. That is 319px on a 375px screen and 264px at 320px. Phone lists have no width cap, so a foldable or small tablet under 40rem gets lists up to about 583px. One list per screen matters more. Snap starts each list at the gutter. A sticky strip of list names with card counts sits above the carousel. A colored list gets a 0.5rem dot in its strong step before its name, and an uncolored list gets none. Tapping a name scrolls to that list, the list in view gets `aria-current`, and the strip ends in a + button that jumps to the add-list slot. Only the board scrolls sideways at 320px, apart from the strip's names.

### Sizes

Controls are 2.25rem tall and icon buttons and chips are 1.75rem. On `pointer: coarse` both rise to 2.75rem. Labels sit 0.375rem above their inputs, fields sit 1.125rem apart, and dialog sections, dialog padding, and dialog footers use 1.75rem.

Use `scroll-padding` wherever sticky chrome sits over scrolling content, so a focused card is never hidden under a header or the list strip.

### Named rules

**The One Density Rule.** There is no compact mode. Only the coarse-pointer rule changes sizes.

**The 320 Rule.** At 320px wide every list fits on screen, and every list keeps a focusable element, so the sideways board still passes reflow. The phone strip's names are the one other thing that scrolls sideways. The strip stays one row, because the board's `scroll-padding` needs its fixed height, and it scrolls the current name into view as the board moves. The + stays pinned outside the scrolling names.

### Named rules

**The Step Order Rule.** In dark mode the order is always board (L 0.165), then list (about 0.2), then card (0.245). A list tint never makes a list lighter than its cards.

**The Straight Card Rule.** A card only tilts while it is picked up: `rotate(-1.5deg) scale(1.02)` with `--shadow-lift`. At rest it is straight.

## Shapes

Soft, nested corners. An inner radius is the outer radius minus the padding between them, so a 0.875rem card in a list with 0.5rem padding gives a 1.375rem list. The focused list's lime header uses the list radius minus the list padding, which is 0.875rem.

- Chips, badges, counts, and avatars: full pills (999px).
- Buttons, inputs, and selects: 0.75rem.
- Cards and covers: 0.875rem. The card clips its cover, so a cover has no radius of its own.
- Lists: 1.375rem.
- Dialogs, popovers, and menus: 1.5rem. The members panel rounds only its corners that face the board.
- Multi-line fields cap at 1rem, so a textarea never turns into a pill.

The 0.875rem card radius is the same as shadcn's nova card on purpose. Laneway reads as its own because of the 1.375rem lists, the 1.5rem dialogs, the pill chips, and the roomier padding. Don't change the card radius to make it stand out.

### Icons

Hugeicons' free stroke-rounded set (`@hugeicons/react` with `@hugeicons/core-free-icons`), named as `iconLibrary` in `components.json` ([ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-hugeicons.md)). Its soft corners match Instrument Sans and the radii. Icon sizes are in rem. The stroke is 1.5px as rendered at every size, so `strokeWidth` is `1.5 * 24 / size` in px. The table gives the values at the default text size.

| Role      | Size    | `strokeWidth` | Use                                                                       |
| --------- | ------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `meta`    | 1rem    | 2.25          | Card footer: due, description, comments, attachments, checklist, watching |
| `control` | 1rem    | 2.25          | Icon buttons, buttons with a leading icon, Add a card, search             |
| `nav`     | 1.25rem | 1.8           | Sidebar nav and Create board                                              |

The free set is stroke only. An on state, such as a starred board, fills the stroke glyph with `fill="currentColor"`. Every icon is `aria-hidden`, and the control or visually hidden text beside it carries the name.

### Buttons

Calm and solid. 0.75rem corners, 2.25rem tall, 0.875rem inline padding, 2.75rem on a coarse pointer.

- **Primary:** lime fill with dark olive text. One per view at most.
- **Secondary:** `secondary` fill with `foreground` text. Share in the board header uses it.
- **Ghost and icon:** no fill at rest and the faint lime `accent` on hover. Icon buttons are 1.75rem.
- **Focus:** a visible ring in `ring`, which is olive in light mode and lime in dark mode, at 3:1 or better against what it sits on.

### Chips and labels

- A named label is a pill in the color's tint with same-hue text from the table above. It shows its name on the card face and everywhere else.
- A color-only label is a small swatch pill in the strong step with no text. Its edge reaches 3:1 on `card` and `muted`. Yellow swatches in light mode and Black swatches in dark mode get their ring.
- Labels never collapse to bare color bars.

### Card

The signature component. Straight, quiet, and only as tall as its content.

- **Order:** cover, labels, title, meta. Each part shows only when the card has it, 0.5rem apart. That includes the gap under a cover, so a covered card's content starts 0.5rem below the cover, not at the 0.75rem top padding.
- **Title-only card:** just the title at 0.75rem by 0.875rem padding. No minimum height and no compact form.
- **Color cover:** a 1.75rem band of the strong step across the top, with no text. Yellow in light and Black in dark draw their ring as a 1px inset line on the band's bottom edge.
- **Image cover:** edge to edge at 16:9, `object-fit: cover`, cropped from the center.
- **Meta:** one wrapping row of meta text with 1rem icons: due date, checklist, description, comments, attachments. A late due date reads "Nov 18, overdue" in `destructive`. A complete due date, and a checklist with every check item checked, read in `success`. Description is an icon alone with hidden text "Has a description". The rest are an icon plus a count, each with hidden text.
- **Menu button:** top-right corner. With a fine pointer it shows on hover and focus. On a coarse pointer it is always visible. Over a cover it gets a `card` background and `--shadow-card`.
- **Grip:** a separate grab handle button inside the card, always focusable. It shows on hover and focus with a fine pointer, and only on keyboard focus on a coarse pointer.
- **Picked up:** `--shadow-lift` and `rotate(-1.5deg) scale(1.02)`. Its slot stays behind as a placeholder filled with `foreground` at 9%, at the card radius.

### List

- `list` surface, tinted by its color as described under Colors. An uncolored list keeps plain `list`.
- 1.375rem radius, 0.5rem padding, 18rem wide on the desktop board.
- The list that holds focus fills its header with lime and dark olive text, on top of any tint.
- "Add a card" is a full-width button at the bottom. It opens an inline composer in place. Enter adds the card to the bottom and Escape cancels.
- The add-list control is a ghost list after the last list, and the last snap stop on a phone.

### Moving cards

- A mouse drags the whole card. Touch drags after a 250ms long press.
- Enter on a focused card opens card detail. Space on the grip picks the card up. `restoreOnCancel` is on, so Esc puts it back.
- Announcements name the card and list, never ids: "Picked up Fix login in To do, position 2 of 4", "Moved Fix login to Done, position 3 of 5". Lists dragged by their handle get the same treatment.
- "Move…" in the card menu and in card detail opens list and position selects. It is anchored with a fine pointer and a bottom sheet on a coarse pointer. It is the WCAG 2.5.7 alternative to dragging and the sure way to move between lists on a phone.
- Check items follow the same rules, with "Move up" and "Move down" in their menu.

### Card detail

A centered dialog up to 35rem wide from 40rem up, full screen below 40rem. 1.5rem radius, 1.75rem padding, `--shadow-dialog`.

- **Cover:** bled to the dialog's edges above the title. A color cover is a 2.5rem band of the strong step. An image cover is 16:9 and stops at 12rem tall, so it never becomes a big image cover.
- **Labels:** a picked label is a chip. A color-only label shows its swatch plus the color name, unlike the card face's bare swatch, so the chip says what Backspace will remove.
- **Due date calendar:** a day cell is `--size-control`, but shrinks to a seventh of the viewport less 3rem when that is smaller. That keeps the 2.75rem coarse cells inside 320px. It is the one width set in `vw`.

### Inputs and fields

- 2.25rem tall, 0.75rem radius, `card` background, a 1px `input` border that reaches 3:1.
- Focus: the `ring` color.
- Error: `destructive` border and message text.
- Textareas cap their radius at 1rem.

### Navigation

The sidebar uses `sidebar`, 1.25rem `nav` icons, and lime `sidebar-accent` with dark olive text on the current board.

### Members

- **Avatar:** 2rem initials disc in one of the four moss steps, initials at 40% of the size and weight 600. Every avatar is `aria-hidden`, because a name always sits beside it or in the button's label.
- **Stack:** in the board header, 2rem avatars overlapping 0.375rem, each with a 2px `background` ring. Three faces, then a +N disc in `muted` with `muted-foreground` text. Four members show all four. The stack is one button, "Board members, N", at control height.
- **Share:** a `secondary` button beside the stack, icon and label from 40rem up, icon only below 40rem. It shows only to people who may add members.
- **Members panel:** 22.5rem wide. At 64rem and up it sits beside the board, and the board narrows instead of sitting under it. From 40rem to 64rem it slides over the board's right side with a scrim, and the board keeps its width. Full screen below 40rem. `popover` surface with `--shadow-popover`. Members group under Admins, Normals, and Observers with counts, and an empty group hides. A row has the avatar, the full name, a "you" marker, and "@username" below. Guests get an outlined "Guest" pill titled "Not a member of this workspace".
- **Role change:** only an admin sees it. Each row is a "Change role" button with `aria-expanded` that opens a radio group:
  - Admin: "Changes anything, including members and board settings."
  - Normal: "Adds, edits, and moves lists and cards."
  - Observer: "Reads the board but can't change it."
    The last admin's radios are disabled, with "Make someone else an admin first. A board always needs one."
- **Observer board:** every editing control is hidden, not disabled: "Add a card", the "Add a list" ghost, the grip, the list menu, and the card menu's Move…, Edit labels, and Archive. The card menu keeps Open card and Copy link. A `role="note"` strip on `secondary` above the lists says: "You're an observer. You can read this board and comment on cards, but you can't change it. To edit, ask an admin to change your role:" followed by the admins' names. The comment clause shows only when observers may comment. Dismissing hides it until the next load, and nothing is stored.

### Mascot

It is always decorative: `aria-hidden="true"` and an empty `alt`. The heading and text beside it carry the whole message. [ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-mascot-raster-from-masko.md) records why it is raster art from masko.ai.

- 404 and "can't read this board" share one screen, so the app never reveals that a private board exists.
- Stills are transparent PNGs, named like `cobble-pleased.png`. The one clip is WebM with an HEVC fallback for Safari. It plays once in under 5 seconds and rests on its last frame. Nothing loops.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, show the PNG of the clip's last frame and never fetch the video.
- Pointing ships facing right. CSS mirrors it with `scale: -1 1` when the action sits on the left.
- A size is the edge of the square image box, transparent padding included. masko.ai exports squares, so every pose lines up without a per-pose crop.
- Hero is `min(12rem, 30svh)` from 40rem up and `min(8rem, 30svh)` below. That's 192px and 128px when height isn't tight, and the phone size keeps sign-in's heading, fields, and button above the fold on a 375×667 phone. The `30svh` cap is for phones in landscape. It can take the hero under the small size there, which is accepted for decorative art.
- Small is 7.5rem at every width. It's the smallest size the drafts were checked at, so going smaller needs a fresh check.
- Stills ship at 384px and 768px wide as a `srcset` through `next/image`. The clip stays at 720px.
- Seven expressions plus the neutral base is the cap. A new placement reuses one before anyone adds an eighth. Failure screens (puzzled, apologetic, waiting) stay gentle, never comic.

### Motion

Short, spatial, and never bouncy. Things that arrive or move use the enter ease. Things that leave use the exit ease. Nothing uses a spring or overshoot, and durations don't scale with screen size.

| Token             | Value                        |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `--duration-fast` | `120ms`                      |
| `--duration-base` | `200ms`                      |
| `--duration-slow` | `250ms`                      |
| `--ease-enter`    | `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` |
| `--ease-exit`     | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` |

| Transition                     | Motion                                                                                                                                  | Reduced motion                                                   |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Card hover                     | Swap to `--shadow-card-hover`, fast                                                                                                     | Instant                                                          |
| Card lift                      | To `--shadow-lift` and the tilt, fast, enter ease. The placeholder appears at once                                                      | Shadow and tilt apply at once                                    |
| Card drop                      | Back to rest, base                                                                                                                      | Instant                                                          |
| Move… to another list          | The card slides to its new slot, slow. Neighbors close the gap, base. Then a lime wash, `primary` at 50% into `card`, fades out over 1s | The card leaves and reappears with no travel, plus the same wash |
| Undo a move                    | Same as Move…, back to the old slot                                                                                                     | Same as reduced Move…                                            |
| Dialog open                    | Fade plus scale from 0.96, base. The scrim fades over the same time, linear                                                             | Fade, fast                                                       |
| Dialog close                   | Fade plus scale to 0.98, 150ms, exit ease                                                                                               | Fade, fast                                                       |
| Tooltip                        | Opens after a 300ms hover delay, so moving across a board doesn't flash tooltips. Fade plus scale from 0.95, fast                       | Fade, fast                                                       |
| Popover and menu               | Fade plus scale from 0.95 out of the trigger corner, fast. On a coarse pointer the sheet rises 1.5rem instead                           | Fade, fast                                                       |
| Toast                          | Rises 1rem with a fade, base. Leaves with a fade, fast                                                                                  | Fade, fast                                                       |
| Phone list jump from the strip | Smooth scroll to the list's snap point                                                                                                  | Jumps, no scroll                                                 |

Reduced motion is one rule everywhere: nothing moves through space and nothing scales. Overlays only fade, at most 120ms. The lime wash stays, because it is a color change and it is how you find a moved card. The edges get no extra motion beyond the one welcoming clip.

### Left to the build

The tickets behind this file handed these to the build. Where a ticket suggested a default, use it.

- Popover and bottom sheet close: fade out at `--duration-fast` with `--ease-exit`, matching the dialog.
- dnd-kit's drop animation: `--duration-base` with `--ease-enter`.
- Focus return when the members panel closes, and Escape from inside an open row: follow Base UI's dialog defaults.
- How long a toast stays. Base UI pauses it on hover and focus, which a toast with Undo needs for WCAG 2.2.1. The prototype used 6s.
- Whether a touch drag can carry a card across lists with edge auto-scroll, or only within a list. "Move…" is the sure way between lists either way.
- Whether dark popovers and dialogs also step one level lighter than `card`, beyond the `border` ring.
- How the icon stroke holds at 1.5px when the browser's text size grows the rem-sized icons.
- The invite, add, leave, and join flows. They live in the members panel.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** put dark olive text (`primary-foreground`) on every lime fill.
- **Do** use the olive `ring` for focus in light mode and lime in dark mode.
- **Do** keep board, list, and card in that lightness order in both themes, with no card border.
- **Do** nest radii: 0.875rem cards, 1.375rem lists, 1.5rem dialogs, pill chips, 0.75rem controls.
- **Do** raise every control, icon button, and chip to 2.75rem on `pointer: coarse`.
- **Do** use rem for layout and size, and px only for hairlines, rings, shadows, and icon stroke.
- **Do** give every drag a "Move…" alternative and announce moves by card and list name.
- **Do** show a label's name, or its color name in the tooltip of a swatch.
- **Do** hide editing controls from observers instead of disabling them.
- **Do** keep every icon, avatar, and mascot `aria-hidden` with a name beside it.
- **Do** keep the mascot to empty or blocked screens, and keep its copy sincere on failure screens.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use Geist, Lucide, zero-chroma grays, a black primary, or shadcn's faint gray focus ring.
- **Don't** use purple gradients, glassmorphism, Corporate Memphis people, sparkle AI icons, colored icon tiles, or a different hue per stat tile.
- **Don't** tilt a card at rest or use big image covers.
- **Don't** put lime text on a light surface or use lime for decoration.
- **Don't** add a second typeface, a mono, or viewport-only fluid sizes.
- **Don't** add a compact density.
- **Don't** put the mascot on a board with cards, in a dialog, toast, or banner, or loop its animation.
- **Don't** name Cobble in UI copy.
- **Don't** let a label collapse into a bare color bar.
- **Don't** use lane markers, street signs, or other laneway motifs in the board chrome.
- **Don't** mix in components from a `radix-*` style.
